## Specific uses of AI
In this writing task, I only utilised AI for two types of auxiliary work: First, at the very beginning of writing, I asked AI to sort out a basic writing outline and had a brain storming based on the assignment topic, it helped me plan the essay structure, clarify the writing direction and prevent my argument from going off track.
Second, after I finished the full Chinese text, I used AI to assist with Chinese-English translation for the English submission. Nevertheless, I manually proofread and revised all translated sentences to ensure they matched my original intention.

## This writing topic selection: 
Why do designers still need to learn programming when AI code generation tools are popular and anyone can generate all kinds of codes with AI tools?

## The original idea: 
Only by learning the most basic things can you add your own ideas and details on this basis. Only then can you make what you want. Many students think that AI can directly generate code, and it is meaningless to learn to write code by hand, but I want to refute this point of view, because if we don’t master the skill of code, AI generates some wrong code, and we have no way to modify it, so we still need to carry out Systematic learning, master the learning power of creation 

## Filtering ideas from AI suggestions
The course declaration mentioned some directions worth pulling on, When I asked AI for brainstorming directions at the start, it produced a full list of many different arguments for this topic. After reading through all the suggestions, I found putting all these ideas into one essay would make the argument messy and unfocused. Therefore, I only picked several relevant lines of reasoning that fit my core standpoint, and discarded the rest of AI generated ideas to keep my writing tight and coherent.
